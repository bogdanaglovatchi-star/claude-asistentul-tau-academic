# Exercise can improve sleep quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis

*Autori: Masahiro Banno, Yudai Harada, Masashi Taniguchi, Ryo Tobita, Hiraku Tsujimoto, Yasushi Tsujimoto, Yuki Kataoka, Akiko Noda · An: 2018 · Jurnal: PeerJ · DOI: 10.7717/peerj.5172 · PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6045928/*

## Abstract
The review examined exercise effectiveness for patients with insomnia using randomized controlled trials.
Nine studies with 557 participants were included in quantitative analysis. Results showed exercise improved
both subjective and objective sleep measures, though benefits varied by outcome type. The authors concluded
that "exercise can improve sleep quality without notable adverse effects" and recommended inclusion in
clinical practice guidelines.

## Afirmații-cheie cu cifre
- Sinteză cantitativă: „Nine studies with 557 participants".
- Scor PSQI: „MD, 2.87 points lower in the intervention group; 95% CI, 3.95 to 1.79 lower" (dovezi de calitate **scăzută**).
- Scor ISI (insomnie): „MD, 3.22 points lower; 95% CI, 5.36 to 1.07 lower" (dovezi de calitate **foarte scăzută**).
- Eficiența somnului: „MD, 0.56% lower ... 95% CI, 3.42% lower to 2.31% higher" — **nesemnificativ**.
- Latența adormirii: „1.90 min higher ... 95% CI, 3.63 lower to 7.43 higher" — **nesemnificativ**.
- Timp total de somn: „4.32 min higher ... 95% CI, 9.19 lower to 17.84 higher" — **nesemnificativ**.
- „Only four trials noted adverse effects" — efecte adverse minime.

> Nuanță importantă: îmbunătățirea e clară la măsurile **subiective** (PSQI/ISI), dar măsurile **obiective**
> (eficiență, latență, durată) nu au atins semnificația. Material excelent pentru un review critic.
