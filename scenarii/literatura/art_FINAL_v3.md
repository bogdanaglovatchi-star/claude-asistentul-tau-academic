# Relationship between Habitual Caffeine Consumption, Attentional Performance, and Individual Alpha Frequency during Total Sleep Deprivation

*Autori: Quiquempoix et al. · An: 2023 · Jurnal: International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health (IJERPH) · DOI: 10.3390/ijerph20064971 · PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10049386/*

## Abstract
The study examined how habitual caffeine intake affects cognitive performance during total sleep
deprivation. Researchers evaluated 37 subjects in a double-blind crossover protocol using the psychomotor
vigilance test (PVT) with EEG recordings every six hours. Participants received either 2.5 mg/kg caffeine or
placebo twice daily (08:30 and 14:30) during sleep deprivation. The findings indicate that the effect of
acute caffeine on attention and on EEG markers during sleep deprivation is modulated by a person's habitual
caffeine consumption.

## Afirmații-cheie cu cifre
- Eșantion: „37 subjects (15 women, 40.5%)", vârstă medie „30.2 ± 4.4 years".
- Consum obișnuit mediu de cafeină: „212 ± 66 mg/day".
- Grupuri de consum: Low 0–50 mg/day (n=10); Moderate 51–300 mg/day (n=11); High >300 mg/day (n=16).
- Doză acută: „2.5 mg/kg caffeine or placebo twice daily (08:30 and 14:30)".
- Consumatorii „low" au avut timpi de reacție PVT mai scurți vs. „moderate" (p=0.015) și vs. „high" (p=0.049).
- Frecvența alfa individuală „significantly lower in high-caffeine consumers"; consum zilnic mai mare a corelat cu timp de reacție mai mare.
