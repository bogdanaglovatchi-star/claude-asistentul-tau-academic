# Coffee's effects on cardiac arrhythmias, physical activity, sleep and serum glucose: Insights from the Coffee and Real-time Atrial and Ventricular Ectopy (CRAVE) trial

*Autor: Gregory M. Marcus · An: 2023 · Jurnal: Clinical and Translational Medicine · DOI: 10.1002/ctm2.1348 · PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10374881/*

## Abstract
Insights from the CRAVE trial, a randomized, case-crossover study of 100 participants over a 14-day period
with over 1,000 outcome assessments, examining the acute effects of coffee consumption on cardiac
arrhythmias, physical activity, sleep, and serum glucose. Coffee consumption did not affect premature atrial
contractions but was associated with more premature ventricular contractions, more daily steps, and less
sleep; no significant effect on daily mean serum glucose was observed.

## Afirmații-cheie cu cifre
- Design: „randomized, case-crossover trial with 100 participants", „14-day study period with over 1,000 outcome assessments"; vârstă medie „39 ± 13 years; 51% women".
- Cafeaua asociată cu „approximately 50% more premature ventricular contractions (PVCs)"; >1 cafea ⇒ dublarea PVC (semnificativ).
- Repartizare aleatorie la cafea: „1,058 additional daily steps (95% CI 441–1,675, p = .0010)"; per cafea în plus: „587 more steps daily".
- Cafeaua a redus somnul cu „36 minutes nightly (95% CI 25–47, p < .001)"; per cafea în plus: „14 fewer minutes sleep".
- Metabolizatorii lenți ai cafeinei: „~60 minutes less sleep"; cei rapizi: fără diferență detectabilă.
- „No significant differences in daily mean glucose" legate de consumul de cafea.
